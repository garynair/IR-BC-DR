# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution. This list covers **incident response (IR)** and **business continuity / disaster recovery (BC/DR)**: IR standards and planning, scenario playbooks, digital forensics basics, tabletop exercises, the Business Impact Analysis, RTO/RPO-driven DR strategy, crisis communications, and post-incident review.

## What belongs here

- Official IR and BC/DR standards and their supporting documents (NIST SP 800-61/86, ISO 22301).
- Free, ready-to-use playbooks, tabletop exercise packages, and checklists from credible sources (CISA, SANS, FEMA).
- Templates that help a practitioner start an IR plan, BIA, or post-incident review rather than build one from scratch.
- Commercial IR orchestration and BC/DR planning platforms with a named feature set.
- Certification and training paths specific to IR and BC/DR.
- Government and standards bodies that own the frameworks referenced here.

## What does not belong here

- Legal breach-notification timelines — covered by the companion [Privacy Compliance](https://github.com/garynair/privacy) list; reference them, don't duplicate them.
- Offensive/testing tools and methodology — covered by the companion [VAPT](https://github.com/garynair/vapt) list.
- Working exploit code or attack tooling of any kind.
- Vendor marketing content without a substantive free tier, open-source component, or named feature.
- Self-promotion of products you have not used or do not work with.

## How to submit

1. Open an issue titled `[Submission]: Resource Name`, or open a PR directly.
2. Add the entry in alphabetical order within the relevant section, in the format: `- [Name](URL) - one sentence on what it is, one sentence on why a practitioner would care.`
3. If proposing a new template, include a filled-in example, not just an empty structure.
4. If you are the author or maintainer of the resource, disclose that in the issue or PR description.

## Style

- American English in the description copy.
- No emojis.
- Avoid banned vocabulary (delve, leverage, harness, robust, seamless, holistic, transformative, paradigm). Plain language wins.

## Quality bar

- Must be publicly accessible (official source, free tier, or open-source).
- Must be actively maintained (commit within 12 months, or a standards body with an active update cycle).
- Description must be factual.

## Review

A maintainer reviews PRs within seven days. Most PRs that meet the criteria above land within two weeks.
