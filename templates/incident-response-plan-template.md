## 5. Incident Lifecycle (PICERL)

1. **Preparation** — reference this plan, playbooks, and tooling readiness.
2. **Identification** — how incidents are detected and confirmed; initial triage steps.
3. **Containment** — short-term and long-term containment actions; reference relevant scenario playbook.
4. **Eradication** — removing the threat; validating no persistence remains.
5. **Recovery** — restoring systems; validating integrity before returning to production.
6. **Lessons Learned** — reference the post-incident review template.
